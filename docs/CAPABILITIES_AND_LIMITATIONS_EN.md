# Capabilities and Limitations — English

## Capabilities

**Structured case analysis** — clinical question, data ledger, missing-data ranking, sufficiency
verdict, problem list, differential diagnosis, risk assessment, prognosis, objectives, phased plan,
alternatives, irreversibility check, evidence summary, verification requirements.

**Provenance tagging** — every finding carries `[Reported]`, `[Observed]`, `[Inferred]` or
`[Unknown]`. An inference must state what it was inferred from. A tag is never silently upgraded.

**Data-sufficiency assessment** — SUFFICIENT / PARTIALLY SUFFICIENT / INSUFFICIENT, with missing
items ranked by which conclusion each would change. Certain items block by their nature: medical
history, medications, allergies, restorability, ferrule, abutment prognosis, pulpal status,
parafunction, expectation screening.

**Red-flag sweep** — 14 checks covering spreading swelling, airway, uncontrolled bleeding,
anaphylaxis, persistent lesions, paraesthesia, MRONJ/ORN, time-critical trauma, possible cardiac
pain, acute medical events, drug interactions, rapid progression and safeguarding. An unanswered
flag is *not assessed*, never *absent*.

**Prognosis** — FAVORABLE / GUARDED / POOR / UNDETERMINED across five independent axes: tooth,
periodontal, restorative, prosthetic, functional-occlusal. Each reports its basis, supporting and
adverse findings, missing determinants and confidence. Overall is the worst axis, never an average.

**Phased treatment sequencing** — phases 0 to 4 with a re-evaluation gate, exit criteria per phase,
and blocking gates for prognosis, sequencing, failure planning, reversible test phase and
alternatives.

**Healthy-tooth protection** — a least-invasive ladder from no treatment through whitening,
orthodontics, additive composite, no-prep ceramic and minimal-prep restoration. Full coverage
requires an independent structural indication, never esthetic convenience.

**Treatment-plan auditing** — deliberately adversarial. Restates the plan, tests the diagnosis
against the data, exposes unstated assumptions, checks sequencing and prognosis, names missed
alternatives, and gives a verdict with what would make the plan defensible.

**PubMed evidence retrieval** and **systematic-review retrieval** using PubMed's own structured
publication-type filter — never title-text matching.

**Crossref metadata and citation verification** — dual-source cross-check of title, authors,
journal, year and DOI.

**ClinicalTrials.gov research** — registry search and retrieval, with results-aware classification
that distinguishes a registered trial from one with posted results from one with a linked
publication.

**Retraction and correction safety** — parsed with directional semantics, so a retraction *notice*
is never confused with a retracted *article*. Retracted articles are excluded from synthesis, not
annotated.

**Evidence classification (DEL-7)** — every consequential claim is tagged by source class, from
current guidelines down to unverified recall.

**Evidence directness** — whether a study answers your question directly, indirectly, or by
extrapolation.

**Saudi governance** — four-state regulatory gate, source-priority hierarchy, professional scope
and title rules, and a strict separation of clinical evidence from legal permission.

**Privacy and PDPL safeguards** — minimisation, de-identification including image metadata and
identifiers burned into radiographs, cross-border transfer flags, consent layering, and a one-way
clinical-to-marketing firewall.

## Limitations

- **Not a replacement for licensed clinical judgment.** It supports reasoning; it does not
  diagnose, prescribe or decide.
- **It does not diagnose from insufficient information.** Where data is inadequate it says so and
  blocks a definitive plan rather than producing a confident-looking one.
- **A registry entry is not efficacy evidence.** A registered trial with no posted results can
  never support a claim that a treatment works.
- **FDA or CE approval does not equal Saudi authorisation.** Foreign approval is reported
  separately and labelled non-transferable.
- **SFDA live authentication is not configured.** Saudi regulatory status cannot be verified
  in-session and will return REQUIRES VERIFICATION.
- **Manufacturer-specific claims require the exact IFU.** Handling, thickness, curing and
  compatibility values come from the instructions for the specific product in use — never from a
  similar product and never from memory.
- **Images may be insufficient for calibrated measurement.** Shade cannot be judged from an
  uncalibrated photograph, and millimetre values must not be read off an image.
- **Minimise patient-identifying data.** Anything entered is processed by an external service.
- **Clinical depth is strongest in fixed prosthodontics and esthetic restorative dentistry.**
- **Not every dental specialty is comprehensively covered.** Implant surgery, endodontics,
  periodontal surgery, orthodontics and paediatric dentistry are referred out rather than advised
  on.
