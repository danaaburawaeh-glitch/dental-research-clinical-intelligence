## The closure principle

One rule resolved six of the eight: **volatile operational facts leave the governed clinical
document.** A laboratory's name, its contracted turnaround, a product's trade name and a session's
duration all change without any clinical reasoning changing — keeping them in the protocol forced
re-approval on every commercial change and, in v1.2, produced both the duration conflict (item 3)
and the misspelling (item 4). They now live in Appendix B and the new Annex E, which are living
documents that do not block approval.

The other two were resolved by **deleting unsupported numbers rather than sourcing them** — the
protocol's own stated logic.
