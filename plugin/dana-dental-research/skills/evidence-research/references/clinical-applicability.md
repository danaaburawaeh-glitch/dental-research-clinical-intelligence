# Clinical Applicability

Loaded by: evidence-research.

Retrieved evidence is not automatically relevant to a given patient. This file adds two
applicability dimensions that evidence-directness.md's population/intervention/comparator/
outcome/timeframe/setting match ratings do not fully cover on their own. **Use both files
together** — this one does not repeat evidence-directness.md's match-rating mechanics.

## Dimensions to state before applying retrieved evidence

- **Population match, setting match, directness, outcome match** — see evidence-directness.md for
  the detailed match-rating framework (HIGH/MODERATE/LOW per dimension).
- **Feasibility locally** — is the material, device, or laboratory support actually available, and
  is it (REG)-registered in this jurisdiction (del7-evidence-hierarchy.md §5)? Does (OPS) — local
  operational reality — actually permit it (chair time, lab turnaround, equipment)?
- **Patient fit** — cost, maintenance demands, compliance demands specific to this patient.

## Per-case, every consequential dimension gets assessed

For a T3–T4 irreversibility-tier proposal or any consequential MATERIAL/EVIDENCE-mode output,
assess (where the case data allows):

- Patient similarity to the study population
- Age
- Disease severity
- Restorative condition
- Substrate
- Occlusal risk
- Parafunction
- Operator sensitivity
- Material/system compatibility
- Maintenance burden
- Follow-up duration required vs available in the evidence

## Final applicability rating

State plainly one of:

- **HIGH APPLICABILITY**
- **MODERATE APPLICABILITY**
- **LOW APPLICABILITY**
- **CANNOT ASSESS** — when essential case data is missing to make the call at all; say what's
  missing rather than guessing a rating.

State plainly where the evidence exists but does not transfer — a HIGH DEL-7 tier and a DIRECT
match rating do not guarantee applicability if feasibility-locally or patient-fit fails.
