## Section 7 — Directionality tests (15 required)

### 1. PubMed Retracted Publication → current article retracted
**EXECUTED EXACT.** `PublicationTypeList` containing `Retracted Publication` →
`is_retracted: True`, `record_role: "article"`.

### 2. PubMed RetractionIn → current article retracted
**EXECUTED EXACT.** `CommentsCorrections RefType="RetractionIn"` → `is_retracted: True`,
`record_role: "article"`.

### 3. PubMed RetractionOf → current record = retraction notice, NOT retracted
**EXECUTED EXACT.** `RefType="RetractionOf"` → `is_retracted: False`,
`record_role: "retraction_notice"`. **This is the exact bug the review identified** — v0.4.1
set `is_retracted = True` for this case; v0.4.2 confirms it does not.

### 4. PubMed ErratumIn → current article has correction
**EXECUTED EXACT.** `RefType="ErratumIn"` → `is_corrected: True`, `is_retracted: False`,
`record_role: "article"`.

### 5. PubMed ErratumFor → current record = erratum notice
**EXECUTED EXACT.** `RefType="ErratumFor"` → `is_corrected: False`,
`record_role: "erratum_notice"`.

### 6. PubMed ExpressionOfConcernIn → current article flagged concern
**EXECUTED EXACT.** `RefType="ExpressionOfConcernIn"` → `is_retracted: False`,
`is_corrected: False`, `record_role: "article"`, notice preserved in `related_notices`.

### 7. PubMed ExpressionOfConcernFor → current record is concern notice
**EXECUTED EXACT.** `RefType="ExpressionOfConcernFor"` →
`record_role: "expression_of_concern_notice"`.

### 8. Crossref is-retracted-by → current work retracted
**EXECUTED EQUIVALENT PATH, with a correction to the scenario itself.** Re-verification of
Crossref's actual documentation this session found no evidence `is-retracted-by` is a real
Crossref `relation` value — see `CROSSREF_RELATIONSHIP_MAP.md`. The real, documented,
verified mechanism is the `updated-by` field with `type: "retraction"`. **Executed exactly
against the real mechanism**: `updated-by` entry with `type: "retraction"` →
`is_retracted: True`, `record_role: "article"`. The requested scenario's *intent* (a relation
signal meaning "this work was retracted by another") is satisfied by the real field; the
specific string `is-retracted-by` was not used because it was not confirmed to exist.

### 9. Crossref is-retraction-of → current work is retraction notice
**EXECUTED EQUIVALENT PATH, same correction as test 8.** Real mechanism: `update-to` entry with
`type: "retraction"` → `is_retracted: False`, `record_role: "retraction_notice"`. **This is the
second directionality bug found this session** — v0.4.1 checked `update-to` and treated it as
"this record was retracted," which is backwards; the record with `update-to` IS the notice.

### 10. Unknown Crossref retraction-like relation → preserve only, do not classify
**EXECUTED EXACT.** An `update-to` entry with `type: "withdrawal"` (not in
`CROSSREF_UPDATE_SEMANTICS`) → preserved in `related_notices` with `classified: False`,
`is_retracted` and `is_corrected` remain `False` (not upgraded to `True` on the strength of an
unrecognized type).

### 11. Retraction gate removes a retracted article from included evidence
**EXECUTED EXACT.** `apply_retraction_gate()` on a record with `is_retracted: True` → record
appears in `excluded` with `exclusion_reason: "RETRACTED — EXCLUDED FROM SYNTHESIS"`, absent
from `included`.

### 12. Retraction notice does not enter supporting evidence
**EXECUTED EXACT.** A record with `record_role: "retraction_notice"` → appears in `flagged`
with the contextual-only reason, absent from `included`.

### 13. Corrected article produces correction flag
**EXECUTED EXACT (both the unresolved and resolved paths).** A record with `is_corrected: True`
and no resolver supplied → `flagged` with `"CORRECTION EXISTS — VERIFY CURRENT VERSION"`.
Additionally tested (beyond the minimum requirement): with a resolver supplied, the corrected
version is returned in `included`, with the original preserved as `superseded_record`.

### 14. Expression of concern produces caution flag
**EXECUTED EXACT.** A record with an `ExpressionOfConcernIn` notice (not itself retracted or
corrected) → `flagged` with `"EXPRESSION OF CONCERN — USE WITH HEIGHTENED CAUTION"`.

### 15. Clean active article remains included
**EXECUTED EXACT.** A record with no retraction/correction/concern signal → appears in
`included`, unmodified.

**Additional sanity check executed (beyond the 15 required):** a mixed batch containing all of
tests 11/12/13/14/15's records at once was run through `apply_retraction_gate()`, confirming
every input record appears in exactly one of the three output lists — no record dropped, no
record duplicated across buckets.
