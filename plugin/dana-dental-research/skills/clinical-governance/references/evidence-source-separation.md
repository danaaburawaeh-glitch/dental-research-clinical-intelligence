# Evidence Source Separation

Loaded by: clinical-governance, evidence-research, quality-control.

## Source classes (DEL-7 vocabulary) — summary only, see del7-evidence-hierarchy.md for full detail
- (L1) current recognised clinical guideline
- (L2) systematic review / meta-analysis
- (L3) well-designed clinical study
- (L4) consensus / expert guidance
- (LAB) in vitro / bench / computational data
- (IFU) manufacturer instructions for that specific product
- (REG) regulatory clearance/status
- (JUDG) clinician/practice preference — not external evidence
- (OPS) local operational fact
- (KOL) lectures / social media / course content
- (UNVER) unverified / recalled, not retrieved

## Non-negotiable separations
- LAB is not clinical evidence. Bench/in vitro results must not be presented as proof of clinical
  performance or superiority.
- IFU is not comparative efficacy evidence. Manufacturer instructions govern handling/compatibility
  for their own product only.
- Regulatory clearance (REG) is a gate, not evidence of superiority or efficacy relative to
  alternatives.
- JUDG is not external evidence, however experienced the clinician. Local protocol is JUDG/OPS, not
  a literature finding.
- KOL content (lectures, courses, social media) is not evidence.
- AI-generated text is never a source and must never be cited as one.
