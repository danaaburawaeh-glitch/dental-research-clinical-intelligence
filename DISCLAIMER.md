# Clinical Disclaimer

**Dental Research & Clinical Intelligence by Dr. Dana**, version 1.0.0.

This software **supports** clinical reasoning. It does **not replace** the judgment of a licensed
clinician.

## What this software is not

- It is **not a registered medical device**, and nothing in it constitutes a medical-device
  authorisation, clearance or approval statement. It holds no SFDA, FDA, CE or equivalent
  clearance.
- It **does not independently establish a diagnosis**. Any differential, prognosis or plan it
  produces is a structured proposal for a clinician to evaluate, accept, modify or reject.
- It **does not prescribe**. Drug-related output is decision support only, never a prescription,
  and all agents, doses, durations and interactions must be verified against current prescribing
  information and the patient's complete record.

## Known conditions of use

- **Results may be incomplete when data or external services are unavailable.** A failed or
  unavailable literature search means nothing was searched. It is never evidence that no evidence
  exists.
- **Clinician verification is required before any irreversible treatment.** Preparation,
  endodontic treatment, extraction, implant placement and comparable procedures must not proceed on
  this software's output alone.
- **Product instructions for use (IFU) must be verified independently.** Handling, thickness,
  curing, compatibility and contraindication values come from the manufacturer's instructions for
  the specific product in use. They do not transfer between products, however similar.
- **Saudi regulatory status must be verified independently.** The regulatory connector is not
  authenticated in this release, so Saudi status cannot be confirmed in-session and will be
  reported as *requires verification*. Foreign approval — FDA, CE or otherwise — never establishes
  Saudi authorisation.
- **This software must not be the sole basis for emergency care.** Where a red flag, airway
  concern, uncontrolled bleeding, suspected anaphylaxis or comparable emergency is present, act on
  clinical protocol and escalate to appropriate human care immediately.

## Responsibility

Final diagnosis, treatment selection, prescribing, every irreversible procedure and every
patient-specific decision remain the responsibility of the treating clinician. Users are
responsible for compliance with the laws, regulations and professional standards of their
jurisdiction.

The software can be confidently wrong. Everything clinically consequential requires verification
before use.
